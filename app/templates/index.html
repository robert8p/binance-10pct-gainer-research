Step 1 — Complete candidate grid

Every eligible symbol is evaluated at every 15-minute UTC boundary. Entry is the interval's first trade price, not a hindsight-selected low. +10% is tested over the following eight hours. Discovery, validation and sealed test are split chronologically before analysis, with an eight-hour embargo.

Step 2 — Raw evidence export

Exports the complete candidate ledger plus ten days of raw 15-minute history and 48 hours of raw one-minute history. No controls are selected and no predictor features are calculated.
First analysis upload: index + every Discovery ledger, market and subject part. Keep Validation and Sealed Test unopened.
